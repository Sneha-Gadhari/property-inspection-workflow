Property Inspection Agreement

Standards, scope of work, and liability disclaimer

1. Scope of Inspection: This visual, non-invasive inspection covers readily accessible systems and components including structural foundation, roof covering, electrical distribution, interior plumbing, HVAC, and fire egress routes per InterNACHI and ASHI guidelines.

2. Limitations & Exclusions: Latent, concealed defects, underground pipes, environmental hazardous soil analysis, and unpermitted alterations beyond visual line-of-sight are excluded from this baseline scope.

3. Authorization: The client authorizes certified inspectors to enter the designated property, conduct system tests, take documentary photographs, and log municipal compliance reports.
